# 03. Economics and Token

## Protocol Economics

ORBIT is asset-agnostic. Any token on the Zircuit network can be locked into an escrow contract and distributed automatically as campaign rewards. This makes growth campaigns flexible — projects can reward in stables, native ZRC assets, or their own tokens.

A protocol fee (2%) applies to campaigns not funded in $IFL, with value recycled back into ecosystem growth.

## Token Role

Utility: unlocks premium features (analytics, Boosts, extended API capacity).

Fee Reduction: discounted fees when campaigns are funded in $IFL.

$IFL is structured as a MiCA-compliant utility token: functional access, discounts without security-like features. 

## Tokenomics Overview

**Token Name:** Influence Token ($IFL)

**Supply Distribution:**
Campaign incentives (KOLs & players): 50%
Ecosystem fund: 15%
Team & Partners (vested): 15%
Liquidity: 10%
Community / Investors: 10%

**Token Utility:**
Unlock premium campaign features
Fee reduction for campaigns funded in $IFL

**Economic Incentives:**
KOLs and gamers are incentivized through instant payouts, highlights, and special features unlocked via $IFL.
Studios and projects are incentivized to adopt and engage with $IFL to lower costs, increase campaign visibility, and access advanced tools.
High community and ecosystem allocation boosts token circulation and long-term adoption.
