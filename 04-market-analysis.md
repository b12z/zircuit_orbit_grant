# Market Analysis

## Assumptions for Product-Market Fit

Projects need real user acquisition, not inflated vanity metrics. Platforms rewarding likes and follows fail to deliver ROI.
KOLs want trustless payouts and recognition — instant, on-chain claims and visibility for their reach.
Gamers engage more with fun, pump-fun style mechanics than repetitive “follow & retweet” tasks.
Zircuit requires transaction-heavy applications to bootstrap usage and demonstrate network traction. ORBIT aligns with this by driving measurable actions and payouts on-chain.

## Launch Strategy

We combine a cross-platform launch strategy where every piece — KOLs, gamers, minigames, and rewards — is hooked together into one growth loop.

Telegram minigame funnel: Launch through Photon, our Telegram minigame already beta-tested with 4,000 users onboarded at zero marketing budget. This validates low-cost viral growth.
Activate KOLs: Our 300+ KOLs (10M+ audience) drive content and affiliate campaigns, rewarded for bringing in gamers.
Reward gamers: Photon incentivizes users for activity and missions, creating ongoing engagement.
On-chain settlement: Both KOLs and gamers withdraw rewards instantly on Zircuit, becoming ORBIT users and Zircuit users at the same time.

Validated loop → ecosystem scaling: This integrated flow — KOLs → gamers → Photon engagement → on-chain rewards — is already proven. Next step: scale it across every app in the Zircuit ecosystem.

## User Acquisition and Market Strategy

Leverage KOL base: Activate 300+ KOLs with a combined reach of over 10M. Campaigns seeded through their content and affiliate links.
Activate gamers: Photon minigame as entry point to onboard the first large user cohort. Referral mechanics amplify viral spread.
Ecosystem visibility: Partner with Zircuit for ecosystem-wide announcements, case studies, and content across socials — positioning ORBIT as the efficient way to onboard growth.
Q1 seasonal campaigns: Network-wide rewards for active projects, KOLs, and players to sustain momentum and retention.
